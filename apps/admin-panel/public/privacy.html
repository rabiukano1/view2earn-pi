Privacy Policy for View2Earn

Effective Date: August 1, 2026 | Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to View2Earn ("Company," "we," "us," or "our"). We operate the View2Earn mobile application, web applications, and associated backend infrastructure (https://view2aern.org).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information. We adhere strictly to Google AdMob Rewarded Inventory Policies, Google Play Developer Program Policies, Apple iOS Privacy Guidelines (NSPrivacy), GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, and survey partner network terms (CPX Research, BitLabs, Pollfish, InBrain).

2. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Data: Email address, display name, and unique User ID (via Google OAuth or email sign-in).
  • Public Payout Addresses: Voluntary public EVM or Solana blockchain wallet addresses for token distribution. We NEVER collect or store private keys or seed phrases.
  • Support Submissions: Optional verification proof screenshots voluntarily uploaded for support requests.

B. Automated & Anti-Fraud Signals

  • Mobile Advertising Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID) and Apple IDFA (subject to user ATT permission).
  • Device Attributes: Model, OS version, locale, screen resolution, and connection type.
  • Security Signals: Anonymized device signal hashes (system boot time and time zone offsets) used strictly to verify human traffic and prevent bot abuse.

3. How We Use Your Information & Legal Basis

We process your data strictly for legitimate operational and compliance purposes under GDPR Art. 6:

  • Maintaining user point ledgers, daily streak rewards, and instant PIPRO token swaps.
  • Serving voluntary AdMob rewarded video ads upon your explicit request.
  • Routing survey offerwalls and auto-crediting reward webhooks.
  • Detecting and blocking invalid traffic, bot farms, and proxy/VPN fraud.

4. Third-Party Network Disclosures

We do not sell your personal data. Data is shared only with authorized partners:

  • Google AdMob (Google LLC): Ad serving and Google UMP consent framework.
  • Survey Networks: CPX Research, BitLabs, Pollfish, InBrain.
  • Cloud Providers: Convex Cloud, Cloudflare, Vercel.
  • Public Blockchains: Public Solana and EVM ledgers for token payouts.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

Under GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to access, rectify, export, or permanently delete your account and personal data.

To request account deletion, click Profile → Settings → Account Security → Delete Account inside the app, or email privacy@view2aern.org.
