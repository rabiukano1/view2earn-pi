# View2Earn — Product Roadmap

*Last updated: October 2026*

What we have shipped, what we are building, and what we are still weighing.
Items marked **gated** depend on an external approval or partner account, so their timing is
not entirely ours to set. We publish no dates we cannot control.

---

## Now — shipped and live

**Accounts & access**
- Email and password sign-in, email one-time codes, and sign-in with Telegram
- Non-custodial payout addresses — users supply an address; we never ask for a seed phrase or private key

**Earning**
- Task feed: follow a page, join a channel, answer a quiz, complete a survey
- Daily check-in streak, daily quiz, weekly leaderboard, combo bonuses
- Daily bonus features and a learning academy with level quizzes
- Referral programme with a qualification requirement
- Optional rewarded video, always user-initiated

**Verification & trust**
- Multi-stage verification with an automated screenshot check and human review fallback
- Channel-join verification through our Telegram bot — no screenshot needed
- Profile linking with a one-time code, validated against the real profile
- Layered fraud protection: device signals, connection reputation, behavioural checks, risk scoring
- Screenshots are deleted automatically after a short retention period

**Rewards**
- Rewards catalogue with live redemption status
- Automated airtime and data bundle fulfilment, with automatic reversal if delivery fails
- Internal marketplace where users can list their own profile for discovery, subject to review

**Operations**
- Admin review queues for verifications, redemptions, listings and risk flags
- Published policies: Terms, Privacy, Cookies, Anti-Fraud, Rewards & Redemption, Child Safety

## Later — planned, not yet started

- **Marketplace depth** — richer discovery, categories, and campaign controls for listings
- **Reward catalogue expansion** — more bundle sizes and more countries
- **Notifications** — streak reminders, redemption status, leaderboard changes
- **Per-administrator identity** — individual accounts and roles, replacing shared access
- **Sidra Chain ecosystem support** — a separate app for Sidra users, with its own economy
- **Accessibility and low-end device work** — the markets we serve run modest Android hardware
  on expensive data; app size and data use are product features, not afterthoughts

---

## Exploring — no commitment

- **Advertising infrastructure** — we plan to expand our advertising infrastructure to better connect
  brands with an engaged audience
- **Wallet features** — multi-asset deposit and withdrawal, pending legal and regulatory review
- **Developer partnerships** — tooling for other developers in the ecosystems we serve

## How to read this roadmap

Anything under **Now** exists today and you can use it. **Next** is being worked on.
**Later** is agreed in principle but unstarted. **Exploring** may never ship.

We would rather publish a short, true roadmap than a long, optimistic one. Items move between
columns as reality dictates, and this page is updated when they do.
